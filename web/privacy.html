Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: 16 September 2026

About this policy

This policy covers the Purplecoins Android app and its download website, maintained by purplevarun. Purplecoins is a local-first finance and personal organisation app. You do not need to create a Purplecoins account to use it.

The app stores your records on your device. It does not upload those records to a developer-operated server, and we do not sell them or use them for advertising. External services, Android backups and sharing actions are explained below.

Information you enter

Depending on the features you use, the app stores:

  • Financial records, including accounts and money sources, currencies, transactions, categories, trips, budgets, investments and exchange rates.
  • Notes, tasks, folders, due dates and completion status.
  • Password, card and identity records, including the details you choose to enter.
  • Files you attach to supported records, and your app settings.

This information is used to display your records, calculate balances and analysis, organise your information, and provide backup and restore. Purplecoins does not connect to your bank or automatically import bank transactions.

Local storage and security

Records and attachments are stored in the app's SQLite database within Android's app storage. Purplecoins does not add its own database encryption. The Vault name describes a way to organise records, not a promise of encrypted storage.

Protection depends on your device, operating system and security settings. Use a device screen lock and take care when storing passwords, full card details or identity numbers. No storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Backups and sharing

A .purplecoins export is an unencrypted SQLite backup containing the app's records and attachments. Anyone with access to that file may be able to read its contents, including sensitive financial, password, card and identity information.

You choose where to save or share exported backups through Android. A destination app, cloud drive or recipient handles the copy under its own practices. Protect exported files and remove copies you no longer need. Restoring a backup replaces the records currently stored in the app.

Android backup is enabled in the current app configuration. Depending on your device and system settings, Android or your device provider may back up or transfer app data, including through a cloud service. These operating-system features are separate from Purplecoins' manual export. Review your Android backup settings and your provider's privacy policy.

Exchange rates and device features

When you request updated exchange rates, the app contacts Frankfurter. The request includes INR as the base currency and the non-INR currency codes used by your money sources. It does not include account names, balances, transaction amounts, notes or credentials.

Like other internet services, the exchange-rate provider receives connection information such as your IP address and request time. Its handling of this information is outside Purplecoins' control. You can enter exchange rates manually instead of requesting them online.

File selection, backup sharing and copying values use Android's file picker, sharing controls and clipboard when you choose those actions. Information you copy or share can leave the app's storage and is subject to Android's protections and the receiving app's practices.

Website and external services

The download website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The download page also requests public release information from GitHub's API to find the latest APK. Visiting the site, downloading a release, or following source and support links contacts GitHub, which may process IP addresses and other request information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

We do not add advertising cookies, tracking pixels or analytics services to the app or this website. The website cannot read the database in your Android app. External websites, app stores and services have their own privacy policies.

Your choices and data deletion

Your local records remain until you change or delete them, replace them through a restore, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Archiving a record hides it from active lists; it does not delete it. Some linked financial records cannot be deleted while other records still refer to them.

You can export a backup through Settings before clearing data. Android's app settings let you clear Purplecoins' storage or uninstall it. This does not remove exported files, copies sent to other people, or backups held by your operating-system or cloud provider. Manage those copies separately; a later system restore may bring backed-up data back.

The maintainer cannot access, recover or delete the records stored only on your device. Deleting a record is not a guarantee of forensic erasure from device storage or existing backups.

Support and privacy questions

Contact the maintainer through the Purplecoins issue tracker for privacy questions or concerns. If you submit a support request, the information you provide is visible to the maintainer and handled by GitHub.

GitHub issues are public. Do not post backups, passwords, card details, identity documents or screenshots containing personal records. We use information you choose to submit to respond to the request; GitHub controls storage and removal of content on its platform.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or website changes. The current version will remain available on this page with an updated date. Review it before using new features or sharing sensitive information.
